# The Four Surface Design Modes

From **Impeccable**: The design mode names what visitor success looks like on a specific surface. Choose the mode based on the *requested screen*, not the overall product company.

---

## 1. Persuade Mode
- **Surfaces**: Landing pages, marketing websites, product launches, pricing pages, campaigns.
- **Goal**: The visitor decides and acts; design *is* the product.
- **Execution**:
  - Full commitment to an unmistakable visual point of view.
  - Large display typography (median headline ratio 4:1 to 8:1 over body text).
  - High ambition in motion and visual texture.
  - One primary action clearly stated and immediately reachable.
  - Authentic visual assets (no fake stock photos).

---

## 2. Operate Mode
- **Surfaces**: Web applications, SaaS dashboards, editors, admin portals, settings, billing, inboxes, data tables.
- **Goal**: The visitor completes a task with speed and confidence.
- **Execution**:
  - Scanability, consistency, and native affordances outrank expression.
  - Quiet borders, small clear labels, repeated modular panels.
  - No decorative gradients, glows, rainbow charts, or fake vanity metrics.
  - Data density tailored to the user's role (compact tables, clear sorting/filtering).
  - Brand identity lives in refined details: crisp focus rings, haptic button presses, fast keyboard navigation.

---

## 3. Read Mode
- **Surfaces**: Documentation, technical specifications, articles, changelogs, knowledge bases, legal terms.
- **Goal**: The visitor understands and retains complex information.
- **Execution**:
  - Structured for comprehension and effortless long-form reading.
  - Comfortable line length (45–75 characters per line).
  - High typographic legibility: optimal line-height (`1.5` to `1.7`), distinct hierarchy between `h1`–`h4`.
  - Content-rich sidebars, table-of-contents sticky navigation, and clear anchor links.
  - Avoid intrusive popups or disruptive scroll animations that hinder reading.

---

## 4. Experience Mode
- **Surfaces**: Portfolios, creative agency showcases, interactive exhibits, art galleries.
- **Goal**: The visitor is immersed inside the work itself.
- **Execution**:
  - The artifact leads from the first viewport; navigation and interface chrome recede into the background.
  - Full-bleed imagery, subtle ambient audio/video loops, and custom scroll choreography.
  - Experimental layouts (Z-axis cascades, horizontal galleries) permitted when deliberate.
